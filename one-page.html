1
TRANSACTIONAL PHASEcard 1
A ceasefire and three deals
Agree the three deals as the agenda, then redeclare the ceasefire across all fronts, Gaza and Lebanon included
  • Hormuz standstill · freeze the maritime conflict · mutual stand-down: the US naval blockade lifts, Iran’s attacks on the strait halt · nobody formally concedes sovereignty, transit rights, or economic arrangements
  • Nuclear bargain · three gates: IAEA access, HEU downblended in place, custody of the rest · $6B, $5B, $15B released as each closes
  • Reconstruction tranche · $25B of Iran's frozen assets · 25% to Gaza & Lebanon, matched dollar for dollar by the Board of Peace · on agreement of the architectural phase, the wartime escalation unwinds
2
END OF WARcard 2 · card 3
The All-Fronts Hudna
The permanent termination of war promised in MoU Art. 1 · US, Iran, and their respective allies
  • A 10–15 year armistice · the Axis disengages from Israel and US forces
  • Reciprocal non-aggression · the US guarantees Israel's compliance; both pursue their interests through the architecture
  • Trump's Comprehensive Plan as the pathway for Palestinian self-determination, with no competing end-state
3
ARCHITECTURAL PHASEcard 2
Five institutions, five trades
Iran performs, a regional institution verifies, money and sanctions relief move, and the exchange settles with finality
  • The Table for the war itself · the Consortium, nuclear · the Commission, Hormuz · the Forum, arms and presence · the Fund, reconstruction
  • Sanctions relief and asset releases · the compensation and compliance layer beneath all five
  • The Board of Peace inaugurates the institutions and hosts the signing · the region governs thereafter
4
POLITICAL SETTLEMENTcard 3
What each side actually commits
Every track is an exchange · settled exchanges ideally stay settled over a crisis on another track
  • Nuclear · near-zero enrichment for 10–15 years, one protected research cascade below 1%, and zero HEU · the right to enrich recognized, then a supervised ramp to a 1.5–3.67% ceiling
  • Hormuz and arms · the strait reopens, sovereign rights acknowledged · restraint on missiles, American presence, and regional arsenals
  • Reconstruction · a 25% carve-out after the nuclear gates, roughly $20B, matched by the Board of Peace · a ~$500B regional program
5
REGIONAL DÉTENTEcard 4
~15 years · the waypoint
Fifteen years without major conflict, alongside real if uneven progress across the five tracks
  • Major incidents are expected · the institutions exist to keep them from cascading into another regional war
  • Near-zero enrichment gives way to the supervised ramp
6
REGIONAL NORMALIZATIONcard 4
20 to 30 years · the capstone
Diplomatic relations among all the states of the region, with the end of US-Iran enmity as the keystone
  • Not warmth · a channel that holds under stress · every capital can reach every other in an emergency · a Middle East that secures itself
  • Earned on the cumulative record · generated by the institutions, not claimed
