The core thesis

The Islamabad MoU was a skeletal framework for a deal without a destination. Card 5 · limits 1–2 of 7

What the Architecture Cannot Do

1 · It needs Trump

  • This runs on one man's personal diplomacy
  • Nobody else has the appetite to own it or the standing to move the region
  • The architecture is built to outlive him, but nothing gets built without him

2 · Nothing is truly irreversible

  • Irreversibility is an ideal, constrained by politics
  • Every element can be undone by a party willing to pay the cost
  • What the architecture changes is how slow, how visible, and how damaging reversal becomes
  • Finality has chargebacks, and geopolitics files more of them than finance does
  • And finality covers acts, not conditions: compliance is continuous, so a party that stops performing ends the series, and ending the series can undo much of what it was building

CARD 5 · LIMITS

X.COM/THEKINGDAVIDJR

Card 5 · limits 3–5 of 7

What the Architecture Cannot Do

3 · No central enforcement

  • The institutions negotiate standards and produce receipts, not adjudications
  • Enforcement is left to the distributed anarchy of geopolitics

4 · A weak security guarantee

  • Nobody is trapped in this
  • Staying has to be worth more than leaving, and that is the only thing holding it together

5 · Sanctions relief primarily American

  • Reality is that relief primarily runs through Washington's political calculations
  • What the architecture supplies is a pathway Washington and others can follow, and regional partners with their own reasons to press for it

CARD 5 · LIMITS

X.COM/THEKINGDAVIDJR

Card 5 · limits 6–7 of 7

What the Architecture Cannot Do

6 · The fault lines are entrenched

  • Israeli-Palestinian, Sunni-Shia, US-Iran, Arab-Persian, Islamic-Western
  • The architecture can only attempt to contain them

7 · Détente is mutual, not promised

  • Nothing guarantees regional actors won't undermine the pace of progress, or won't decide that détente and normalization aren't in their long-term interests
  • It's a north star, nothing more

The architecture operates at the margins, where the worst outcomes are stumbled into.
