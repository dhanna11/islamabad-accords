Need: Gaza $71.4B, final Rapid Damage and Needs Assessment by the EU, UN and World Bank, 20 Apr 2026 (https://www.un.org/unispal/document/final-gaza-press-release-20apr26/); Lebanon ~$11B, World Bank RDNA, 7 Mar 2025 (https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/03/07/lebanon-s-recovery-and-reconstruction-needs-estimated-at-us-11-billion), before the 2026 fighting, so the real combined need is higher. The 2026 war (from 2 Mar 2026) has no needs assessment yet: direct material damage $3–4B, preliminary, excluding economic losses (Information Minister Paul Morcos via NNA, 6 Jul 2026, https://www.anews.com.tr/middle-east/2026/07/06/lebanon-says-direct-damage-from-israeli-war-estimated-at-3-4-billion); PM Salam told parliament on 15 Sep 2026 that the two wars cost more than $20B and may exceed $27B (QNA, https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2026-9/16/lebanons-pm-cost-of-latest-two-israeli-wars-exceeds-usd-20-billion). Funding at a $100B pool (asset formulas): $17B pledged at the Board of Peace donor conference (FDD, 20 Feb 2026); carve-out 25% of the $74B released after the $26B nuclear gates = $18.5B; Board of Peace match $18.5B. The first tranche's $6.25B is inside the $18.5B, not on top. Total $54B of ~$82B, about 66%. At a $120B pool the carve-out and match are $23.5B each and the share rises to about 78%. Of the $17B pledged, ~$280M had been transferred by 23 Sep 2026. A second match of Iran's $18.5B from non-Board of Peace members in Europe and Asia would bring the total to $72.5B, about 88%, leaving ~$9.9B.
